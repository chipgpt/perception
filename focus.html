WELCOME TO PERCEPTION

Guess. Reveal. Get sharper.

Mixed five picks five different puzzle types. Each daily five is repeatable for your device’s date; Practice makes a fresh mix. Pick an individual game to focus on one skill.

3D view: remember the view, then drag the shape to turn it and drag the green handle around the outer ring to tilt it. Balance: move a support beneath a uniform shape. Line memory: look at a generated path for three seconds, then trace it in one stroke on the blank canvas. Perspective: place the vanishing point. Proportions: resize a rectangle to match the ratio. Each reveal shows the target. All games are silent.

Colour memory: look at a swatch for two seconds, then find it on the wheel. Hue goes around the wheel; saturation increases towards the edge. Arrow keys or the keyboard controls also work.

Scoring: every round is worth 100 points. We divide your error by the game’s zero-point bound, then square the remaining closeness. An error of 10% earns 81 points; 25% earns 56; 50% earns 25. Scores are rounded to whole points. Five rounds total 500 points.

Zero-point bounds

3D and angles: 180°. Balance: 50% of the shape’s width. Line memory: 20% of the canvas diagonal (100 units). Perspective: 40% of the diagonal (200 units). Time: 3 seconds early or late. Rhythm and proportions: 1.5 times or two-thirds of the target. Colour: straight-line distance of one wheel radius. When?: 50 years early or late. How long?: four times or one-quarter of the target duration.

Rhythm: watch five silent flashes, then tap five beats at the same pace. Angles: rotate a line to the target angle. Both reward closeness.

When? Tap to pin an event’s year. Drag through the years and tap to place a pin. How long? Turn the dial from seconds to days. Arrow keys adjust; Shift makes finer changes. Both award up to 100 points for closeness, with the answer and source shown after you commit.

Time: Start, wait, Stop. Points fall with your timing error using the same curve as every other game.

Switching away resets an active memory flash or timer.
