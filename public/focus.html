WELCOME TO PERCEPTION

Guess. Reveal. Get sharper.

Daily Five is the shared daily challenge: five different puzzle types, worth 500 points. Open Practice to choose a skill or play a fresh mix. Individual games are practice-only.

3D view: remember the view, then drag the shape to turn it and drag the green handle around the outer ring to tilt it. Half & Half: cut all the shapes into two equal visible areas. Line memory: look at a generated path for three seconds, then trace it in one stroke on the blank canvas. Perspective: place the vanishing point. Proportions: resize a rectangle to match the ratio. Each reveal shows the target. All games are silent.

Colour memory: look at a swatch for two seconds, then find it on the wheel. Hue goes around the wheel; saturation increases towards the edge. Arrow keys or the keyboard controls also work.

Scoring: every round is worth 100 points. We divide your error by the game’s zero-point bound, then square the remaining closeness. An error of 10% earns 81 points; 25% earns 56; 50% earns 25. Scores are rounded to whole points. Five rounds total 500 points.

Zero-point bounds

3D and angles: 180°. Half & Half: 20 percentage points from an even split (70/30); a 50/50 split earns full points. Line memory: 20% of the canvas diagonal (100 units). Perspective: 40% of the diagonal (200 units). Time: 3 seconds early or late. Rhythm and proportions: 1.5 times or two-thirds of the target. Colour: straight-line distance of one wheel radius. When?: 50 years early or late. How long?: four times or one-quarter of the target duration.

Rhythm: watch five silent flashes, then tap five beats at the same pace. Angles: rotate a line to the target angle. Both reward closeness.

When? Scroll the year wheel beneath the centre marker to choose an event’s year. Up and down arrows adjust by one year. How long? Turn the dial from seconds to days. Use minus, plus or arrow keys to fine tune; Shift and arrows adjust more broadly. Both award up to 100 points for closeness, with the answer and source shown after you commit. Daily five uses each trivia fact once. Trivia practice draws only from past Daily Fives, with up to five different questions per session. Today’s and future questions stay out of the practice pool.

Time: Start, wait, Stop. Points fall with your timing error using the same curve as every other game.

Switching away resets an active memory flash or timer.
